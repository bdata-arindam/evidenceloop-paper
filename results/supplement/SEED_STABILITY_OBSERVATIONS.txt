TWENTY-SEED STABILITY RUN — OBSERVATIONS, 29 OCTOBER 2026
Recorded before any change to the manuscript, supplement or response.

WHAT WAS RUN
  Everything held fixed except the seed, which controls both the train/test split and the
  classifier: 20 seeds, 7 budgets, 2 arms. Features depend on the retrieval and the budget
  rather than the seed, so they were computed once and shared. Seed 0 reproduced both
  published arms exactly, which is the check that the harness is faithful.

1. THE DIP THE REVIEWER POINTED AT IS RESAMPLING VARIATION
   Seed 0 puts the annotator-first minimum at budget 50. Across 20 seeds the minimum lands
   at budget 1 nine times, at 5 three times, at 20 three times, at 50 three times, at 10
   once and at 100 once. Seed 0's pattern occurs in 3 of 20. R3.4 can now be answered
   positively rather than declined.

2. BUT A DIFFERENT NON-MONOTONICITY IS SYSTEMATIC, AND THE PAPER CURRENTLY DENIES IT
   Paired within seed, in the annotator-first arm:
       1 ->   2   +0.0102   20 of 20 seeds improve    sign p <0.0001
       2 ->   5   -0.0068    1 of 20 seeds improve    sign p <0.0001
       5 ->  10   -0.0008    7 of 20                  ns
      10 ->  20   -0.0014    6 of 20                  ns
      20 ->  50   +0.0011   13 of 20                  ns
      50 -> 100   +0.0045   14 of 20                  ns
   The arm genuinely peaks at two sentences and genuinely falls from two to five. That is a
   real non-monotonicity; the paper says the non-monotonicity is not interpretable.
   The feasible arm does NOT do this: its 2 -> 5 step is +0.0041 with 15 of 20 improving
   while its maximum is at budget 100 in 14 of 20 seeds; the drop therefore belongs to the oracle
   intervention rather than to the budget.
   A mechanism consistent with that contrast, NOT tested here: once the annotator's
   sentences are placed first, reading further adds only non-gold sentences that dilute
   the summary statistics the verifier reads. Offered as a hypothesis, not a finding.

3. THE PAPER'S QUOTED ORACLE GAPS COME FROM A FAVOURABLE SPLIT
   paper value against the distribution over 20 seeds:
      budget   1   paper +0.0253   seeds mean +0.0197 (sd 0.0040)   85th percentile
      budget   2   paper +0.0168   seeds mean +0.0159 (sd 0.0049)   70th
      budget   5   paper +0.0130   seeds mean +0.0049 (sd 0.0047)   95th
      budget  10   paper -0.0016   seeds mean +0.0031 (sd 0.0036)   15th
      budget  20   paper -0.0032   seeds mean +0.0016 (sd 0.0038)   15th
      budget  50   paper -0.0021   seeds mean -0.0008 (sd 0.0052)   50th
      budget 100   paper +0.0019   seeds mean -0.0011 (sd 0.0036)   80th
   The paper's budget-5 gain sits at the 95th percentile of what resampling produces, while
   its "nothing measurable from ten sentences on" rests on two values at the 15th
   percentile. Across seeds the gap declines smoothly and stays slightly positive at 10 and
   20 rather than vanishing. The direction of the paper's story survives; the sharp
   "all of it below five sentences, nothing after" does not.

4. THE CENTRAL RESULT IS UNAFFECTED AND IS CONFIRMED
   Feasible arm, 1 -> 100: +0.0276 across seeds with 20 of 20 improving, against the
   +0.0280 the paper reports. The headline that evidence recall rises about forty points
   while the decision rises under three is unchanged by resampling.

5. ONE MORE ITEM TO REVISIT
   The paper calls the annotator-first 1 -> 100 difference not separable from noise
   (+0.0046, interval -0.0073 to +0.0163). Across seeds it is +0.0067 with 16 of 20
   improving, sign p 0.0118: weakly positive rather than absent.
